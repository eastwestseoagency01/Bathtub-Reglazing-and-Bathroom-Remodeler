What Bathtub Reglazing Really Means

Reglazing, also called refinishing or resurfacing, is the process of restoring a fixture with a professional grade coating. We begin by cleaning the surface thoroughly to remove soap scum, oils and residue. Next we repair chips, cracks and rough areas, then prepare the surface so the coating can grip. Finally we spray on the new finish in controlled layers. The end result is a smooth, glossy and durable surface that resists stains and is easy to clean. Because the work happens in your bathroom, there is no need to remove the tub, cut into walls or call in a plumber.

Why Reglazing Beats Replacement

Replacing a tub sounds simple, yet it rarely is. You pay for the new tub, then for installation, plumbing changes, tile repair and disposal of the old unit. The job can take days and turn your home into a work zone. Reglazing costs far less, usually finishes in hours, and keeps heavy fixtures out of the landfill. That is better for your wallet and better for the environment. You can also change the color during the process, so an outdated tub becomes a modern white or another shade you prefer.

Materials You Can Feel Good About

The coatings we use are low VOC, bleach resistant and formulated with zero isocyanates. That means less smell during and after the job, a more comfortable experience for your family and a finish that stands up to regular cleaning. Caring for Your Reglazed Surfaces

A reglazed surface is easy to live with when you treat it gently. Use a soft cloth or sponge with a mild, non abrasive cleaner for daily upkeep, and avoid scouring pads or harsh scrubbers that can wear down the finish. Wipe up standing water, fix dripping faucets that leave marks and address any small chip early so moisture cannot creep underneath. We share these tips at the end of every appointment, and you are always welcome to call us with questions long after the job is done.

Bathtub Chip Repair and Cove Repair

Bathtub chip repair is one of the most common calls we receive. A dropped bottle or a heavy shampoo tin can knock a chip out of the enamel and leave a dark, rough spot. Left alone, the exposed area can rust and stain the surrounding finish. We clean the spot, fill it with a strong repair material, level it and refinish the area so it blends with the rest of the tub.

We also fix the bathtub cove, the curved joint where the tub meets the wall. This area often cracks, gaps or grows mildew because water sits there. Repairing and resealing the cove helps keep moisture out of the wall and gives the tub a clean, finished edge. Chip and cove repairs are quick, affordable and often completed along with a full reglaze for the best match. Contact us today and we will restore the edges and corners of your tub.
